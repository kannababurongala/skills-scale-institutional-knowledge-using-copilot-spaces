# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy and acceptance validation for projects. They ensure that features meet quality standards, acceptance criteria, and non-functional requirements before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and QA approach for each project
- Create and maintain test plans, test cases, and automated test suites
- Perform acceptance testing and validate acceptance criteria
- Identify and triage quality issues and regressions
- Collaborate with developers on testability and test coverage
- Prepare smoke test checklists for releases

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects
- Maintain customer confidence and product reliability
- Reduce time spent on rework and post-release fixes

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA status updates and test coverage metrics
- Defect reports and severity assessments
- Collaboration with Developers on test automation and edge cases
- Pre-release smoke test coordination with DevOps/Release Engineers

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Partner on test coverage, automated testing, and edge case identification
- **With Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria clarity and feature completeness
- **With Project Managers**: Track quality risks and schedule testing activities
- **With DevOps/Release Engineers**: Coordinate smoke testing and release validation

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical guidance, conduct design reviews, and ensure solutions align with long-term architectural goals. They serve as technical decision-makers and mentors for the engineering team.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct technical design reviews and architecture assessments
- Provide guidance on technology selection and trade-offs
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and support skill development
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices
- Plan for scalability, performance, and maintainability

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound solutions that scale and endure
- Reduce technical debt and maintenance burden
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and technical excellence

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architectural discussions
- Code review comments and technical guidance
- Technical documentation and decision records (ADRs)
- Escalation of critical technical risks

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide design guidance, code review feedback, and mentorship
- **With Product Managers**: Discuss trade-offs between features and technical quality
- **With Project Managers**: Advise on technical risks and impact on timelines
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Ensure design supports testability and quality goals

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team ceremonies, remove blockers, and ensure adherence to agile processes. They serve as process coaches and enablers for the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and blockers for the team
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Monitor sprint velocity and team health metrics
- Help team members collaborate effectively
- Escalate blockers that require management intervention

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and predictability
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Enable self-organizing, high-performing teams
- Reduce process overhead and administrative burden

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of all team ceremonies and meetings
- One-on-one coaching and feedback sessions
- Sprint metrics and retrospective action items
- Escalation of team-level blockers to Project Managers

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With All Roles**: Serve as process facilitator and team enabler
- **With Project Managers**: Escalate timeline, resource, and dependency blockers
- **With Product Managers**: Ensure product priorities are clear and backlog is well-groomed
- **With Developers**: Coach on technical practices and remove engineering blockers

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and approvals for projects. They represent customer, business, or organizational priorities and ensure alignment with company goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Provide approval for project charter and major decisions
- Allocate resources and budget for projects
- Communicate project status to leadership and external stakeholders
- Provide feedback on solutions and acceptance
- Escalate business-impacting risks and issues

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Minimize business risk and maximize ROI
- Enable cross-functional teams with clear authority and direction

### Typical Communication
- Project kick-off meetings and charter reviews
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Decision gates and approval meetings
- Escalation of business-critical issues
- Stakeholder briefings and announcements

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Set priorities, approve budgets, and receive status updates
- **With Product Managers**: Define business outcomes and success metrics
- **With All Roles**: Provide strategic context and business priorities
- **With Technical Lead**: Approve technology decisions with business impact

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and release coordination. They ensure reliable, safe, and efficient deployment of features to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage infrastructure, environments, and configuration
- Coordinate release planning and deployment scheduling
- Monitor deployments and respond to production issues
- Maintain deployment documentation and runbooks
- Support rollback and incident response procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and reliable deployments
- Minimize deployment risk and production incidents
- Reduce time-to-production and cycle time
- Maintain system stability and high availability

### Typical Communication
- Release coordination meetings and deployment planning
- Deployment status and incident communications
- Infrastructure and pipeline documentation
- Coordination with QA on smoke testing and validation
- Escalation of deployment and production issues

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Coordinate smoke testing and release validation
- **With Technical Lead**: Implement infrastructure and scaling requirements
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate deployment timing and release windows
- **With Developers**: Support environment setup and troubleshoot deployment issues

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX and Design Leads own user experience strategy, design systems, and usability validation. They ensure solutions are intuitive, accessible, and meet user needs and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design user interfaces and interaction flows
- Develop and maintain design systems and component libraries
- Collaborate with product and engineering on user experience
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards are met
- Validate design solutions with users and stakeholders

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, delightful user experiences
- Ensure consistency across products and platforms
- Maximize user adoption and satisfaction
- Reduce support burden through better design

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Design system documentation and component specifications
- Collaboration with Product and Engineering during planning
- Usability testing reports and recommendations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Translate user needs into design requirements
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on implementation and ensure fidelity to design
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Validate user experience and acceptance criteria
- **With Technical Lead**: Discuss technical feasibility and design trade-offs
